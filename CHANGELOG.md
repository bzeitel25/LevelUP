## v0.24.0 — 2026-10-15 (build 2026-10-15.2)

Ascended classes, the last tier of the Class Compendium. 98 classes in all.

- **9 Ascended classes:** one for each path at Lv 50 (about 250 hours in that path), plus the Polymath for any 3 paths at Lv 25 each.
  - Virtuoso (Art, Support): Standing Ovation, +30% damage and 25% faster attacks for 6 s, and a 10% heal.
  - Warlord (Body, Striker): Warcry, a 2.5× hit, then +25% damage for 5 s.
  - Archmage (Mind, Controller): Time Stop, the monster skips its next 2 attacks and takes a 1.5× hit.
  - Master Artisan (Craft, Guardian): Masterwork Bulwark, 40% less damage for 5 s and 2 blocked hits.
  - Technarch (Tech, Striker): Singularity, a drone that zaps for 0.6× a second for 6 s, and a 1 s stun.
  - Luminary (Spirit, Support): Radiance, a 20% heal and 1.5 s of no damage.
  - Grand Steward (Life, Treasure Hunter): Grand Ledger, a 1.5× hit and +15% gold from the fight.
  - Archdruid (Nature, Controller): Wild Growth, the monster attacks 50% slower for 5 s while you heal 15%.
  - Polymath (any 3 paths, All-rounder): Renaissance, your best path skill twice, then a 1.5× hit. Carries 4 path skills.
- **Glowing gear sets:** each Ascended class has a 4-piece set in pearl and starlight with the strongest shimmer (400 gold a piece). The full set gives half again its role's bonus.
- **Ascended portrait frames:** one per class, unlocked with the class.
- **Ascended in the class tree:** a new group at the bottom, with progress toward each.
- **Suggest a class:** a form at the bottom of the class panel asks for a name, the real skills it's built on, a role, a signature skill idea and why it fits you. The best ideas become official with "Suggested by" credit.
- Perks share the talent caps like every other class, and classes still never add XP.

## v0.23.0 — 2026-10-15 (build 2026-10-15.1)

Veteran Reserve follow-ups.

- **406 new veteran-level tips:** every skill gets 2 more tips at Expert, Master and Grandmaster, the tiers veteran hours unlock. Category tip sets get one more at Master and Grandmaster. Safety tips come first for trades and sports, as always.
- **"Still practicing?" per skill:** pick when a skill's timer checks in (1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 hours) in its details. - **Class unlocks stay on real hours:** decided that classes keep counting real minutes you log, so boosts never speed them up. The class panel now says so.

## v0.22.0 — 2026-10-14 (build 2026-10-14.1)

Pets join the fight.

- **New Pet slot:** one pet goes in your hero's Pet slot like a piece of gear. Pick it under Gear on your hero or in the Pets panel, or leave the slot empty. Any hero can use any pet, whatever the class.
- **Every pet works the same way:**
  - A set bonus to one stat, like gear.
  - One always-on passive.
  - One battle skill. It sits next to your skills and you tap it the same way, with its own cooldown.

  Pets don't level. Their hits and skill grow with your hero's stats, so training yourself is what makes them stronger.
- **Six categories:** Beasts, Arcane, Constructs, Spirits, Elementals and Oddities.
- **Design your own beast:** a beast you design to look like an animal in your life unlocks when its Animals skill reaches Lv 3 (about an hour). It gives +4 to its stat, and the old pet tricks are now passives with no level gate.
  - Dog: +Might, Guard (blocks one hit per fight), Tackle (a hit that stuns).
  - Cat: +Focus, Pounce (its attacks can stun), Swipe (three scratches).
  - Rabbit: +Fortune, Sniff (+5% chest gold), Zigzag (dodge the next 2 attacks).
  - Bird: +Inspiration, Fetch (+3% battle gold), Dive (a hit that boosts your next 2).
  - Reptile: +Instinct, Cheer (a heal when your HP drops low), Sun Bask (a heal over time).
- **14 more pets to find:** Dragon Whelp, Flying Tome, Arcane Owl, Clockwork Critter, Scout Drone, Pebble Golem, Paint Sprite, Spirit Wisp, Paper Crane, Ember Newt, Moss Sprite, Lucky Coin, Pocket Slime and Mimic. Each has its own pixel art, stat bonus (+2 to +12, bigger for harder ones), passive (crit, dodge, shorter cooldowns, max HP, relic luck and more) and skill (shields, burns, turrets, stuns, heals and more).
- **Dragon hatchlings:** five rare baby dragons that hover beside you, flapping their little wings, each with a crest in its element's color. The Star Hatchling is still peeking out of its starry egg.
  - Ember Hatchling: an egg in the Ember Forge.
  - Frost and Storm Hatchlings: eggs in the Sky Citadel.
  - Jade and Star Hatchlings: eggs in the Endless Spire.

  Each win in those dungeons has a 1 in 20 chance of an egg, and you're sure to have one by your 20th win there. An egg hatches after 3 hours of practice, so you raise it by training yourself. Hatchlings have the biggest stat bonuses (+7 to +18) and strong passives and skills: Puff of Flame, Frost Breath, Static Zap, Healing Mist and Starbreath.
- **Where to find the others:**
  - Path levels: Tome, Drone and Crane at Mind, Tech or Spirit path Lv 15 combined.
  - Classes: Paint Sprite, Clockwork Critter and Spirit Wisp.
  - Dungeon drops: Lucky Coin, Moss Sprite, Arcane Owl, Ember Newt, Dragon Whelp and Mimic. Each win has a 1 in 5 chance, and the pet is yours by your 5th win in that dungeon. Wins you already have count.
  - The shop: Pocket Slime for 250 gold and Pebble Golem for 600.
- **Pets panel on the Hero tab:** your Pet slot, then every pet by category with how to get it. The skill book lists your pet's skill.
- Beastmaster, Packleader, Wildheart and Outrider make any pet fight at full strength: double auto-attack power and +50% skill damage.

## v0.19.0 — 2026-10-13 (build 2026-10-13.1)

Veteran Reserve and honest sessions.

- **Veteran tiers:** pick how much experience you bring in, from Dabbler (10–50 h) to Lifelong (10,000+ h), each with a plain example. A tier counts its bottom by default. In a skill's settings, **Help me estimate** works it out from years, hours a week and years off, without going above the tier you picked.
- **Veteran Reserve:** veteran hours now fill a per-skill reserve of bonus XP that real practice spends: +50% under 500 hours, +100% up to 2,000, +200% beyond. The reserve holds exactly the hours you brought in (up to 10,000), so bigger veterans climb faster, not longer. No practice, no bonus.
  - It stacks with rested XP and elixirs, and each one is spent separately.
  - It never counts as logged time: keys, quests, milestones, class unlocks and hour achievements still use real minutes only.
  - Raise your hours anytime to add to the reserve. Lowering them only removes what's unspent.
  - Existing veteran hours fill the reserve automatically.
- **Reserve on the XP bar:** a tinted stretch after your XP shows how far the bonus carries you, like rested XP.
- **Coming back:** "I used to do this, but it's been a while" keeps the beginner tips on as a refresher. Otherwise tips follow lifetime hours (veteran plus logged).
- **Caught Up:** when the hours you log in a skill match your veteran hours, you get a celebration and a new achievement (150 gold and a Medium elixir).
- **Still practicing?** After 3 hours the timer asks. With no answer in 15 minutes it pauses itself where it asked, so a forgotten timer stops counting.
- **Save review:** timer sessions of an hour or more open a quick review. Log all of it, or keep part and drop or bank the rest to the Veteran Reserve.
- **Edit for 24 hours:** trim any session for 24 hours after saving, and drop or bank what you trimmed. Boosts it used come back.

## v0.11.0 — 2026-10-08 (build 2026-10-08.8)

Elixirs, quests and achievements.

- XP elixirs: drink one from the Inventory and your next practice minutes earn bonus XP, the same way Rested XP works. One works at a time.
  - Small: +5% for 60 min.
  - Medium: +10% for 60 min.
  - Large: +15% for 60 min.
  - Grand: +25% for 60 min.
  - The sheet shows the active elixir and how many minutes it has left. Sessions boosted by an elixir are tagged, and deleting one gives its elixir minutes back.
- Where elixirs come from: Small ones turn up in any dungeon now and then (4% to 10% per win) and half the time when you finish all of a day's quests. Medium, Large and Grand are very rare drops in the deeper dungeons (Grand: 0.2% to 0.5% in the last two), and rewards for weekly quests and big achievements.
- Daily quests: 3 small goals each day, like "Practice for 30 minutes", "Practice 2 different skills" or "Clear a dungeon". Each pays 30 gold, and finishing all 3 adds 50 gold and a 50% chance at a Small elixir.
- Weekly quests: practice 3 hours and clear 5 dungeons. Each pays 150 gold, and finishing both gives a Medium elixir (sometimes Large).
- No FOMO: rewards arrive on their own (nothing to claim), quests simply refresh, and missing a day costs nothing.
- 37 achievements in the Hero tab: hours practiced, skill and total levels, days practiced (any days, never in a row), deep-focus sessions, variety, lessons, dungeon clears, the Endless Spire, trophies, relics, gear, talents, quests and elixirs.
